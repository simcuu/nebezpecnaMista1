When do dark classes end?

Data table
  • Occurrence = one class on one date for one student. A weekly lecture with 40 students over 13 weeks counts 520 times.
  • The data covers a sample of students. Compare shares between buildings rather than absolute counts. Buildings with fewer than 5 sampled students are not included.
  • This is the timetable, not real attendance. It has no weather data, and dusk is calculated for one point for the whole city. Monthly totals also depend on how many teaching weeks the month has (January is the exam period).
  • BO, DK, OV and PV are not on the map because they have no coordinates in places.csv. None of them has any dark classes.
